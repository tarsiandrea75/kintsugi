## Calibration (stress split, before this plan)

`replay-20261030T170115Z-local` (thinking off) and `replay-20261030T173156Z-local` (thinking on), 3 recordings each, report cap 4096 and deadline 2700 s so that neither binds:

| Thinking | Report calls | Longest call | Completion tokens per sample | Sample time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| off | 5 (2 samples needed a second attempt) | 527 s | 318-592 | 533-711 s |
| on | 3 | 801 s | 268-413 (as Ollama reports them) | 662-803 s |

The settings this fixes:

- Deadline per report call: 1200 s for both settings. The longest call was 801 s; the lab holdout-1 ran about 35% slower than dev on the same model (`odd/tasks/benchmark-v0.md`), and 801 s × 1.35 ≈ 1080 s. Two attempts at 1200 s fit the 45 min incident budget.
- Report cap: off keeps Config A's 512, so only the model changes (a one-call sample wrote 318 tokens; the two-call samples wrote 577 and 592 in all, so about 300 per call). On gets 4096, because thinking tokens count against `num_predict` and the reported count may not include them; the cap must not cut the thinking.
- Config A ran with 600 s. No A dev sample came near it (max 435 s), so the longer qwen deadline does not favour qwen on these cases; the difference is stated, not hidden.

## Metric

- AC@1 per fault and pooled with Wilson 95% CIs; coverage and selective accuracy (coverage as fixed in `d4de651`: a timed-out or invalid row has no answer).
- Paired against A and against 0: discordant cases, exact McNemar with Holm, cluster bootstrap; per-fault net computed case by case.
- Report time per sample, and the share of samples that needed a second report attempt.

## Decision rule

ADR-015's dev keep rule, against A: a qwen config is worth a holdout read only with a net gain of at least 3 of 15 cases over A and no fault class losing more than 1 case net. Against 0: descriptive only. If both Qoff and Qon pass, the higher pooled count goes forward; on a tie, Qoff (cheaper). If neither passes, the local route's next step is the hybrid of M3, and qwen is not read on any holdout.

```
infra/scripts/replay-agent.sh --route local --mode decision --split dev --view tables --report plain \
  --model qwen3.5:9b --think on --report-cap 4096 --report-timeout 1200

python3 -m kintsugi_evals score \
  --label 0 results/2026-10-30-config0-dev/inputs/0-summary.tsv \
  --label A results/2026-10-25-m0-dev/inputs/A-summary.tsv \
  --label Qoff ../infra/scripts/out/<think off run> \
  --label Qon ../infra/scripts/out/<think on run> \

```

Check each fresh `summary.tsv` first: 15 samples, split `dev`, view `tables`, report `plain`, and the model, think, cap and deadline columns as in the table.
