## Metric

- AC@1 per fault (F1, F2, F3) and pooled over the 15 cases, each with a Wilson 95% CI.
- Abstentions (`UNKNOWN|UNKNOWN`) and coverage. R-NONE answers are abstentions.
- Paired, 0 against A and 0 against D: the discordant cases each way, the exact two-sided McNemar test with the scorer's Holm adjustment, and the per-fault net.
- Complementarity, descriptive: the cases where exactly one of 0 and A is right, and the AC@1 an oracle that picks the right one of 0 and A would reach. This is an upper bound for a rules-plus-model hybrid, not a result of one.
