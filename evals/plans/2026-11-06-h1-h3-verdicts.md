## Hypothesis

Quoted verbatim from `docs/vision.md` §1:

- **H1, the LLM beats rules.** On the RCAEval holdouts, the paired 95% CI of service-level AC@1, D minus Config 0R, lies above 0. This is the primary test; A minus Config 0R is secondary, and D minus Config 0 and A minus Config 0 are reported as secondary too. If the upper bound is below +5 points, the result reads "rules are enough". The baseline moved from Config 0 to 0R before any RCAEval holdout was read and before D or A ran on RCAEval ([ADR-022](../../docs/adr/ADR-022-h1-baseline-0r.md)).
- **H2, local is enough.** All three must hold, otherwise local is not enough on this hardware:
  - the paired 95% CI of AC@1(A) − AC@1(D), pooled over lab holdout-2 and the RCAEval holdouts, has its lower bound above −10 points;
  - A's false-alarm rate is at most D's plus 10 points;
  - A's median time to report on this laptop is at most 15 minutes.
- **H3, escalation pays.** C stays within 5 points of D while escalating at most 50% of cases.
- H1–H3 get a Holm correction, because three tests at once raise the chance of a false win. Everything else is labelled exploratory. The verdict procedure (one-sided bootstrap p-values against each margin, intersection-union for multi-condition hypotheses, Holm at 0.025) is fixed in [ADR-031](../../docs/adr/ADR-031-h1-h3-verdict-procedure.md).

## Metric

The procedure of ADR-031, restated:

1. **One one-sided test per condition**, whose null hypothesis is that the margin is not met:

   | Condition | Holds when | Null |
   |---|---|---|
   | H1 | D − 0R > 0 | ≤ 0 |
   | H2a | A − D > −10 points | ≤ −10 points |
   | H2b | false-alarm rate A − D < +10 points | ≥ +10 points |
   | H2c | median time < 15 minutes | ≥ 15 minutes |
   | H3a | C − D > −5 points | ≤ −5 points |
   | H3b | escalation rate < 50% | ≥ 50% |

2. **The p-value** is the share of bootstrap resamples whose statistic falls on the null side of the margin, a tie counting on the null side.
   - 10000 resamples.
   - **Seed: 20261106**, fixed now. Every condition's bootstrap starts from it.
   - The unit is the case: a case's repeats move together, and a case weighs once, by the mean of its repeats (D's 3 cloud repeats; C on an escalated case).
   - Pooling over tracks resamples within each track and keeps each track's size.
   - Each condition also reports its point estimate and its two-sided 95% bootstrap percentile CI.
3. **Alpha is 0.025, one-sided**: an unadjusted condition passes exactly when its 95% CI clears its margin.
4. **Intersection-union.** A hypothesis holds only if every condition holds; its p-value is the largest of its conditions': H1 = p(H1); H2 = max(p(H2a), p(H2b), p(H2c)); H3 = max(p(H3a), p(H3b)). A condition with no case has p = 1.
5. **Holm over {H1, H2, H3}.** Sort the three p-values ascending and compare them with 0.025/3, 0.025/2 and 0.025 in turn; at the first that is not below its threshold, it and every larger one fail. Familywise alpha 0.025, one-sided.

**Secondary results, outside the family** (ADR-031 Decision 7, ADR-022), reported without Holm and labelled secondary or exploratory: A − 0R, D − 0 and A − 0 on each RCAEval track; every per-track split of the pooled conditions; the exact McNemar tests and the scorer's own Holm in each track's `score` report; the lab's full-answer table; the C tables computed by `escalate` on D's first repeat.

## Decision rule

Written before any holdout is read (ADR-031 Decision 6):

- A hypothesis **holds** when Holm rejects its null. Otherwise it **is not supported**, and that is reported.
- **H1's readings:**
  - H1 holds: the LLM beats rules on the RCAEval holdouts.
  - H1's 95% CI upper bound is below +5 points: the result reads **"rules are enough"**, whatever Holm decides (both are reported if both apply).
  - H1 not supported and its upper bound at or above +5 points: inconclusive; neither claim is made.
- **H2** holds only with all three of H2a, H2b and H2c; if it is not supported, local is not enough on this hardware, and the failing conditions are named.
- **H3** holds only with both H3a and H3b. A holdout escalation rate above 50% fails H3 on cost, and is reported as such (ADR-030).
- Every outcome is reported in `docs/benchmark.md`; rules beating the model is a finding.
- No config, rule, margin, seed or track changes after any holdout is read. A changed rerun is exploratory.

## Command

From `evals/`, after the four track results are committed:

```
H2=../evals/results/<date>-holdout2-lab
A=../evals/results/<date>-holdout-a-re2ob
B=../evals/results/<date>-holdout-b-re2ss
TT=../evals/results/<date>-holdout-tt-re2tt
python3 -m kintsugi_evals verdict \
  --input holdout-2 A $H2/inputs/A-summary.tsv --input holdout-2 D $H2/merged/D.tsv \
  --input A 0R $A/merged/0R.tsv --input A A $A/merged/A.tsv --input A D $A/merged/D.tsv \
  --input B 0R $B/merged/0R.tsv --input B A $B/merged/A.tsv --input B D $B/merged/D.tsv \
  --input TT 0R $TT/merged/0R.tsv --input TT D $TT/merged/D.tsv \
  --input A-healthy A $A/merged/healthy-A.tsv --input A-healthy D $A/merged/healthy-D.tsv \
  --input B-healthy A $B/merged/healthy-A.tsv --input B-healthy D $B/merged/healthy-D.tsv \
  --service-only holdout-2 --service-only A --service-only B --service-only TT \
  --service-only A-healthy --service-only B-healthy \
  --h1-track A --h1-track B --h1-track TT \
  --h2b-only A-healthy --h2b-only B-healthy \
  --seed 20261106 --iterations 10000 \
  --holdout A,B,TT,holdout-2 --plan plans/2026-11-06-h1-h3-verdicts.md \
  --out ../evals/results/<date>-h1-h3-verdicts
```

- The lab's 0R is not an input: it enters no hypothesis, and `verdict` would read it for nothing.
- `--service-only holdout-2` follows the human decision that the lab is scored on the service in the pooled conditions; it does not change H2b, since a healthy case's rule ignores the scope.
- `verdict` refuses to overwrite `verdict.tsv` or `verdict.txt`, and records each input's sha256, the seed, the resamples and C's rule in `verdict.txt`. It refuses holdout rows without `--holdout` and `--plan`, and writes them to `provenance.tsv` with the plan's sha256 and the commit (ADR-015).
- Check before the run: every input exists, its rows match its track plan's count, and `check-freeze.sh` passes. Since 2026-11-07 the pseudo-case summaries are inputs too: tracks A and B's `merged/healthy-A.tsv` hold 60 and 90 rows, and their `merged/healthy-D.tsv` 180 and 270, every one healthy; their fault-case files keep their original counts (ADR-034). `verdict.txt` names the H2b-only tracks.

**How `docs/benchmark.md` carries the verdicts.** `kintsugi_evals benchmark` renders a verdict directory (one holding `verdict.tsv`) as an "H1–H3 verdicts" section: per hypothesis the intersection-union p, the Holm-adjusted p and the decision; per condition the estimate, 95% CI and unadjusted one-sided p; the seed and resamples; and the `provenance.tsv` line. The verdict result directory is committed with `verdict.tsv`, `verdict.txt` and `provenance.tsv`, and the document regenerates from committed results alone, the four track results and the verdict last, from `evals/`:

```
python3 -m kintsugi_evals benchmark --out ../docs/benchmark.md results/2026-10-30-holdout1-lab \
  results/2026-10-31-qwen-holdout1 <the four track result dirs, each with its healthy/ dir where it has one> \
  results/<date>-h1-h3-verdicts
```
