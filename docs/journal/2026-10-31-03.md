## Decisions and why

- **The scope is the agent module only.** `mcp-tools`, `knowledge`, the scripts and the lab operate the live lab, not the offline track.
- **The orders are derived, not listed in the file:**
  - leaf first by depth, with ties broken by display order;
  - the gather order is its reverse;
  - the causes are the services, then the datastores, then `UNKNOWN`.

  An RCAEval topology file then needs only names, edges and owners. The rule reproduces every lab constant.
- **Pins before the refactor.** The system messages, the four schemas and the gathered lists were snapshotted on the unchanged code, then held byte-identical through T4.
- **T3 kept as one slice of about 1,100 lines.** It is mostly mechanical. Splitting it would leave a branch that does not compile.
