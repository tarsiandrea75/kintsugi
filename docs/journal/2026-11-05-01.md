## Decisions and why

- **The cut was the context window, not the cap.** In HT, prompt plus completion was exactly 8192 in all 5 cuts. The human chose a per-run option over raising the default, because ADR-010 rejected a larger default.
- **The default window stays 8192.** HC gained 1 case, but its slowest sample, 2,540 s, fired the 1,800 s deadline rule.
- **L3 rule (b), post hoc** (the human's choice). The dev data motivated the rule, and ADR-028 says so. It was picked over the transitive, any-section, deepest-service and ratio rules because it gives the fewest correct drops, 4, and has no wrong answer left at L3.
- **The decoys are decoys only.** ADR-020's map is unchanged, so no truth changes. The human confirmed both definitions.
- **The decoys are kept.**
  - The noise check, with repeats and with the first pair left out to avoid regression to the mean, gave a CI whose upper bound is exactly 0. The pre-registered rule therefore says keep, and the human kept them.
  - The probable cost, through more abstentions, is recorded in ADR-029.
- **Plan amendments before any sample.** The cloud cap of 60 samples per run split the noise runs into six. The amendment was committed before the first sample.
