# 2026-10-20 — session 01 (started 2026-10-19)

The evidence dossier (ADR-011) and the cloud development route (ADR-012) were built before this session; their records are `odd/tasks/evidence-dossier.md` and `odd/tasks/cloud-route.md`. This session started from the cloud route's results and closed six features and one fix, all on local branches, nothing pushed.

## What we did

1. **F3 on the cloud route** (`odd/tasks/f3-caller-config.md`, `5b56ade`..`3e1bf58`).
   - An analysis of the 13 recorded F3 incidents ran on the strongest model the subscription offers, Fable 5.1.
   - Two fixes followed. First, an input-validation failure is now an `INVALID_PARAMETER` naming the missing argument. Second, the cause rules were reworded in two steps: a caller that cannot reach a healthy dependency points at its own configuration, and the calls are followed down to the last slow component.
   - F3 on the cloud route went from 0/6 to 3/4. The first rewording broke F1, and the second fixed it.
   - The local confirmation (T6) did not hold: `granite4.1:8b` scored explorer 1/3 and dossier 0/3.
2. **Evidence settling** (`odd/tasks/evidence-settling.md`, `8fd134d`, `c31d253`, `d033a14`). The last restore time is kept on disk, so a run's first fault waits like the others. A cloud round confirmed it: no F1 incident held the previous run's port-9999 lines.
3. **Replay harness** (`odd/tasks/replay-harness.md`, `45f56bb`..`df273c9`).
   - `ReplayLabTools` serves recorded tool outputs through `LabTools`. `record-replay.sh` records runs and refuses key-like strings. `replay-agent.sh` runs the real loop on demand.
   - The harness replays 15 samples in about 4 minutes, for cents.
   - Dossier mode replays fully. Explorer mode does not: it chooses its own arguments, so exact matching serves almost none of its calls.
   - A `/code-review` found 9 issues, all fixed. The most serious: a dossier sample could be served the explorer run's outputs.
4. **Latency in the dossier** (`odd/tasks/dossier-latency.md`, `673aa4a`).
   - On the replay, dossier F1 was 0/5, which made it a systematic miss rather than noise.
   - The cause was missing evidence. The dossier held no latency, gateway-mock answered `200 SUCCESS`, and its 10 s line is INFO.
   - The dossier now reads `query_metric latency_avg` per service. The lab round scored 3/3, and the replay scored F1 4/5, F2 5/5, F3 5/5.
5. **Cut reports on the cloud route** (`fa29d44`). Spring AI passes Anthropic's stop reason as `max_tokens`, while the loop recognised only Ollama's `length`. A cut cloud report therefore got no "cut" retry instruction. Both reasons now count as a cut.
6. **Report length** (`odd/tasks/report-length.md`, `c1fcb9b`..`2fc57c6`).
   - The cloud route has its own answer caps.
   - Two levers were measured separately on the replay: C, a report cap of 768, and B, at most 3 evidence items.
   - Over 15 F3 samples per arm: baseline 14/15, C 13/15, B 10/15.
   - The human kept C and reverted B.

## Decisions and why

- **Prompt-tuning loop stopped.** The human raised the risk of a vicious circle of prompt changes measured on one or two samples. We stopped changing the prompt and moved to deterministic fixes in the evidence (settling, latency), plus a harness that measures many samples. Why: a rate needs samples, and a fact in the evidence holds better than a sentence in the prompt ("the LLM proposes, deterministic code verifies").
- **Explorer measured in the lab, dossier on the replay.** Exact matching cannot serve the explorer's free arguments, and nearest-match guessing would invent evidence.
- **Cloud report cap 768, local 512.** The local cap is set by the CPU's write time; the cloud route has no such limit. 768 removed the cut reports. B was reverted because it cost F3.
- Per-commit reviews ran with standing consent. The stop hook's whole-history candidate (base `49d55bd`) was skipped each time, per the standing preference.
