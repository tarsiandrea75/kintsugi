## Decisions and why

- **Zenodo zips, not the Hugging Face copy.** The Parquet copy puts the truth next to the inputs; the zips are what RCAEval's own scripts download. Both md5s are pinned in ADR-020.
- **The converter writes the existing replay format.** No new dossier source in Java: the gatherer, the rules and the scorer stay the ones every lab measurement used, so a difference between the tracks is a difference in the data.
- **Category map pre-registered** (cpu, mem, disk, socket → `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTION`; delay, loss → `DEPENDENCY_LATENCY`), but AC@1 on RCAEval is service-only, as the published baselines report it.
- **Split hygiene.** Dev is RE2-OB repetition 1. Repetitions 2–3 (holdout A) and RE2-SS (holdout B) are refused by the converter without `--allow-holdout`. Only one RE2-SS header was opened, for the topology, and it is disclosed.
- **Client rows from client spans (T4).** Taking client rows from parent links only lost about 97% of the error signal: calls that fail before reaching the callee leave no child span. This corrected the parent's own brief, not a human decision.
- **Config 0's weakness is expected.** The dossier has no resource facts yet, so cpu, disk and socket faults mostly end as abstentions, and 6 of the 7 wrong answers name a caller of the faulty service.
- **H run although its stop-rule outcome was known.** The rules' candidates capped H at 5/30 against 0's 4/30, so no paired win was possible. The human chose to run it to see how Qon chooses on lists with more than one entry; the plan stated the ceiling and the outcome before the run.
