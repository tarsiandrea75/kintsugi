## Decisions and why

- **`processor_usage`, not `cpu_usage`:** the converter's leakage guard rejects the `cpu` token, and a guard with exceptions is harder to trust than a name (ADR-021).
- **Resource calls left out of replay coverage** (the human's choice): the lab recordings predate them, so counting them would lower every lab replay's coverage without saying anything about the agent.
- **`.git/info/exclude` for the five untracked root files** (the human's choice), so the native review can select no untracked files.
- **HR run although it could not meet the stop rule** (the human's choice, "dai"): to see how Qon chooses on lists with more than one entry. It chose well: 3 of the 4 cases where the rules' first candidate was wrong.
