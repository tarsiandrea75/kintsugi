## Decisions and why

- **V2 is out, whatever the local result.** Under the plan's eligibility rule, a variant must not lose more than one case net on any fault class on the cloud route. V2 loses two on F1 and three on F3.
- **V1's cloud gain proves nothing on its own.** It is +1 case, McNemar p = 1, and the incumbent's 14/15 left at most one case to gain. The plan decides V1 on the local route, where M0 is 5/15: it needs a pooled net gain of at least 3 cases, with no fault class losing more than one.
- **Discard the 14-of-15 run, do not patch it.** A run that is completed by hand is no longer one run under one commit. Rerunning the whole config keeps the input a single `replay-agent.sh` output.
